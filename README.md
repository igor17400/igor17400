## 📖 A Bit About Me

> I'm an AI researcher and engineer who turns machine learning research into production systems. At the University of Tokyo, I worked with Nikkei, owner of the Financial Times, building news recommenders and multi-agent LLM pipelines that mine reader behaviour. That work produced a model that learns from the news readers avoid (SIAM SDM 2025) and NewsReX, an open-source framework that trains news recommenders up to 2.8x faster (CIKM 2026). With Toyota, I researched graph-based recommendation for in-car systems that predicts where drivers will go next. Before that, I led engineering at VOGA through its acquisition by BTG Pactual, Latin America's largest investment bank, and architected a real-time AWS data platform monitoring over USD 300M in client assets. I'm now completing a Master of Research in AI and Machine Learning at Imperial College London, studying why contrastive and mixture-of-experts models collapse when data is scarce.
